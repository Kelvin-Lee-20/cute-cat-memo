💬 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock all 40 cat templates?

Tap any locked cat template in the app to open the unlock screen, then complete the one-time in-app purchase. Once purchased, all current and future templates are yours forever — no subscription, no recurring charges.

I purchased the app but the templates are still locked.

Open the unlock screen and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase. If it still doesn't work, contact us and we'll help.

My memo photo isn't showing up.

Photos are stored locally on your device. Make sure you granted photo library access when adding the photo. If you removed the photo and re-added it, try restarting the app.

How do I save or share my memo?

From the Share screen, tap Share to open the standard iOS share sheet. From there you can save the image to your Photos app, send it via Messages or Mail, or post it to social media.

I lost all my settings after reinstalling the app.

App settings and memo photos are stored on your device, not in the cloud. Reinstalling the app clears them. Your in-app purchase is safe, though — just tap "Restore Purchases" to unlock again.

The app is crashing or acting strange.

Try force-closing and reopening the app, or restart your device. If the problem continues, please email us with your device model and iOS version, and a brief description of what happened.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything runs on your device. We don't collect personal information, and photos you add are stored only locally. See our Privacy Policy for details.
